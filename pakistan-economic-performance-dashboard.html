ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE SCORE
Methodology — each indicator is scored 0–100 (higher = better), converted from the latest reported figure using a simple benchmark scale (e.g. investment-to-GDP against an EM norm, GII rank against the full field). A dimension's score is the unweighted mean of its indicators. The composite Economic Performance Score is a weighted average of the five dimension scores, using the weight sliders in each panel (auto-normalized to sum to 100%). Bands: 0–39 Weak · 40–59 Moderate · 60–74 Solid · 75–100 Strong.

Data — baseline values are drawn from State Bank of Pakistan releases (reserves, Financial Stability Review 2025), Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (CPI, Labour Force Survey 2024–25), the Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26, World Bank poverty/gender data, WIPO's Global Innovation Index 2025, StartupBlink, DataReportal, ILOSTAT and Carbon Brief/Enerdata — current as of August 2026. Hover the source line under each slider for its citation. This is a snapshot, not a live feed: figures update on request, not automatically, since most of these series are published monthly, quarterly or annually rather than in real time.
